International Association for Paranormal Field Research / Registry DSN–03–144

Built for the things ordinary systems leave behind.

Dead Signal Network is an international research association and investigator community. We document unusual reports, protect the people attached to them, and preserve evidence long enough for better explanations to become possible.

Purpose and boundaries

What the network does

DSN exists because unusual experiences are often mishandled twice: first by people who dismiss witnesses, then by people too eager to believe them.

WE INVESTIGATE
  • Recurring environmental and location anomalies
  • Audio, visual, documentary, and physical evidence
  • Witness reports that survive ordinary review
  • Cases requiring multiple professional disciplines
WE DO NOT
  • Publish protected identities or exact unsafe coordinates
  • Treat distress as entertainment or proof
  • Enter sites without an extraction plan
  • Call something paranormal because it is exciting

Founding record

Four receivers. One impossible warning.

In March 2003, four professionals in different fields received the same dead transmission. Its first voice belonged to one of them. Its second segment recorded a conversation they had not yet held.

How DSN works

A professional association with a very loud break room

Casework is structured. Community life is not. Both are necessary.

01Invitation and verification

Existing members may sponsor specialists. Restricted access requires independent approval and retained provenance.

02Teams and disciplines

Field, evidence, cultural, technical, medical, mapping, moderation, and community teams collaborate across cases.

03Roles and distinctions

Official roles show responsibility. Community distinctions recognize useful work, mentorship, translations, top posting, and occasionally surviving a terrible thread.

04Public review

Claims remain open to evidence debate, mundane recreation, correction, and the merciless judgment of Equipment Gremlins.

Membership

A global network built for different bodies and brains

DSN includes members across adult ages, countries, languages, professions, disabilities, and neurotypes. Accessibility is operational infrastructure, not decoration.

4,281MEMBERS ONLINE

67REGIONAL GROUPS

Local knowledge and cultural context lead interpretation. Translation is treated as evidence work.

38ACCESS LANGUAGES

Human-reviewed translation, transcripts, image descriptions, reduced-motion tools, and readable display controls.
