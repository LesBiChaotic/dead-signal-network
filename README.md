# Dead Signal Network

> Document the unexplained. Preserve the evidence. Never investigate alone.

Dead Signal Network is a fictional paranormal research association and community platform. The public-facing site combines a polished professional network, an institutional case registry, analog field equipment, and a gradually emerging horror narrative.

## Canon snapshot

- Founded in 2003 after four specialists received the same impossible transmission.
- Evolved from a small paranormal forum into an international research association.
- The 2019 Wren's End expedition and its seven investigators were erased from official records.
- Wren's End appears in contradictory locations and attaches itself to gaps in maps and memory.
- In 2026, deleted and memorialized accounts have started posting again.
- The site remains useful and credible while its supernatural behaviour becomes gradually undeniable.

## Visual direction

The interface is approximately 75% polished community platform, 15% institutional case system, 8% analog field equipment, and 2% impossible behaviour. Accessibility and readability take priority over atmospheric effects.

## Foundation scope

- Responsive desktop and mobile navigation
- Network dashboard and representative community feed
- Accessible colour, type, motion, and readable-font controls
- Reusable post, case, profile, evidence, badge, and status components
- Structured seed data for the 25 central accounts
- Initial case and feed data
- CSS/SVG identity mark
- Foundation for future Case Registry, Signal Map, Evidence Lab, member profiles, 2003 Archive, and Wren's End progression

## Planned build sequence

This is an original fictional horror project. People, organizations, cases, evidence, and locations presented by the site are fictional.
